Platform 2: Physical docking experiment

The second platform demonstrates physical docking using the planned trajectory and speed reference. Planned docking takes 39.80 s. Excluding 64.96 s of waiting after plan publication, physical docking is completed 45.33 s after sustained motion begins, 5.53 s later than planned.

Within the planned horizon, the follower rear-axle position has nearest-path and time-aligned root-mean-square errors of 0.612 m and 1.822 m, respectively. Imperfect closed-loop speed response and tracking-control errors can contribute to these spatial deviations and timing offsets.
